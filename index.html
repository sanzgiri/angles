Rules to use

  • Triangle angles sum to 180°.
  • Quadrilateral sums to 360°.
  • Pentagon sums to 540°; hexagon 720°.
  • Angles around an interior vertex sum to 360°.
  • Angles on a straight outer edge sum to 180°.
  • Angles at an outer corner of the rectangle sum to 90°.

How to play

Click any dashed grey wedge to type its value. Correct entries lock in green. Fill every angle to win. Press Esc to cancel.
